What this check can and cannot tell you

It spots pressure tactics

Urgency, threats, secrecy, gift cards, requests for codes and passwords. It shows you the exact words it found.

It cannot name the sender

A person can send a scam and a bot can be helpful. Text alone does not show who, or what, wrote it.

No match is not all clear

Scammers reword their messages every day. If a request is unexpected, verify it through a number you already trust.
